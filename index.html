Which Friend of Mine Are You?

A short story-based personality(?) quiz by nicoooo-23

Welcome to the quiz! In this story, you will make choices that will determine which of my friends you are most like. Read each part of the story carefully and choose the option that resonates with you the most. At the end of the quiz, you'll find out which friend you align with based on your choices.

Let's go!

You awaken in an unfamiliar room. The bed underneath you is old. As you sit up—bed creaking with each move—you look around to see that there is no one else present. As you shift, something lumpy presses against your hand. You stand up and check if there is anything underneath. As you lift up the cushion, you see a small wooden box. You pry it open, what do you see inside?

You bring the item you chose with you and head out the room. Outside is a long hallway covered in doors. The wallpapers are peeling and faded. At the end of the hallway is a window—dusty and cracked—where a tiny bit of light seeped through. Behind one of the doors, you hear a faint noise, what do you do?

The noise becomes louder and somehow sounds like it's coming from behind you. You look back to see a creature has followed you. It looks up at you with curious eyes. What is this creature?

You have decided to let the creature follow you as you find a way out of this place. As you both walk toward the end of the hallway, your eyes catch the faint glow of the door to your right. You turn to walk towards it—your hand slowly reaching for the knob. You quickly turn it, hoping to find an exit—but it's locked. You observe the door and notice some carvings on it. What will you do next to unlock the door?

The door has been unlocked. You pushed it open—as the door creaked, the light from outside crept through. You have made it out safely and with a new companion. Thank you for taking the quiz!
